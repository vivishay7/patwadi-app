# Research MOC

## Research classes

- corridor, route and operator availability
- customer pain points and behavioral evidence
- competitor and alternative-service analysis
- pricing, unit economics and willingness to pay
- regulatory and policy research
- technical feasibility and vendor evaluation
- usability studies and support-pattern analysis
- marketing channel and creative research

## Required research-note fields

Record the question, date, geography, method, sample/source, evidence, limitations, findings, confidence and resulting decision or open question.

## Confidence labels

- **observed:** directly seen in current data, code, interviews or operations
- **verified external:** confirmed from a named authoritative source
- **inferred:** reasoned from evidence; explanation required
- **assumed:** working assumption awaiting validation
- **unknown:** no defensible answer yet

## Translation rule

Research does not become product truth automatically. Link the research note to a proposed or accepted entry in [[Decision Log]], then update the affected map: [[Product MOC]], [[Engineering MOC]], [[UI UX MOC]], [[Marketing Brand MOC]], [[Operations MOC]] or [[Legal Compliance MOC]].

Time-sensitive external findings must include an as-of date and should be reviewed before reuse.
