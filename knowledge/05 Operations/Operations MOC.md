## Operating domains

- corridor readiness and serviceability
- pickup, local partner, linehaul and delivery handoffs
- custody proof, exceptions and blocked parcels
- operator onboarding, approval, corridor assignment and availability
- trip creation, transfer, co-conductor and recovery procedures
- customer support, missed attempts, holds, claims and escalation
- settlement, rate cards and reconciliation
- launch checklists, staffing and incident response

## Operational truth rule

A screen capability is not an operating capability until the responsible role, SOP, timing, exception path and evidence are defined. Conversely, a manual pilot process must be labelled manual and must not be described as app automation.

## Change checklist

When an operating rule changes, review all four surfaces:

1. product promise and customer journey
2. app UI and notification copy
3. database/function enforcement
4. policy, SOP and training material

Link resulting changes to [[Decision Log]] and [[Roadmap Releases MOC]].
