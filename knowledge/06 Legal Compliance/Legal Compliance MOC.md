## What belongs here

- customer terms, privacy, shipping, refund and claims rules
- operator agreements, KYC handling and data retention
- prohibited/restricted item rules
- consent, disclosures and app-store requirements
- policy-to-product coverage and identified gaps
- legal review status, jurisdiction and effective dates

## Status discipline

Use exact labels: **working draft**, **approved internally**, **sent for legal review**, **legally reviewed**, **published**, **implemented in product**, and **verified in production**. These stages are independent.

Do not call a template or internal draft legally approved. Do not infer product compliance from the existence of a policy page; use the coverage matrix and verify the relevant screen, database rule or operating procedure.

## Change impact

Every material policy change should be checked against [[Product MOC]], [[UI UX MOC]], [[Engineering MOC]], [[Operations MOC]] and [[Marketing Brand MOC]].
