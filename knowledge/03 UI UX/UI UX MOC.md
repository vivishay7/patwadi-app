## What belongs here

- information architecture and navigation
- screen specifications and interaction states
- booking, payment, tracking and operator task usability
- empty, loading, error, offline and permission states
- accessibility and language requirements
- design-system decisions and component usage
- usability findings, severity and resolution evidence
- content design and customer-facing microcopy

## Required screen-note structure

For a maintained screen or flow note, record:

1. user and job-to-be-done
2. entry and exit points
3. data dependencies
4. primary path
5. alternate and failure paths
6. permissions and role gates
7. copy and trust requirements
8. analytics events, when defined
9. implementation link
10. verification status and date

## Separation rule

A screenshot or mockup is not proof of implemented behavior. Mark artifacts as **concept**, **approved design**, **implemented UI** or **verified flow**. Link engineering enforcement through [[Engineering MOC]] and brand voice through [[Marketing Brand MOC]].
