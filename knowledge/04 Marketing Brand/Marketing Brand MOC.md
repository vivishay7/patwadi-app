## Evidence rule

Keep these separate:

- **Brand rule:** approved and reusable direction.
- **Campaign hypothesis:** what is expected to work.
- **Creative execution:** the actual asset or copy.
- **Performance result:** dated data from a named channel.
- **Learning:** interpretation that may change future work.

Do not turn an untested campaign idea into a brand principle. Do not publish delivery-speed, coverage or performance claims unless the operational source and measurement window are recorded.
