# Refunds, Cancellations & Claims

**Effective:** 14 June 2026

This page explains what happens if you need to cancel a booking, your parcel is delayed, or something goes wrong in transit. It aligns with our [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.html) and [Shipping Policy](shipping-policy.html).

---

## Cancellations

All bookings are prepaid. Refunds go back to your original payment method within **5–7 business days**.

- **Before pickup:** If your parcel has not yet been collected, you receive a **full refund**.
- **After pickup, before the bus leg:** If your parcel has been picked up but has not yet been handed to the linehaul conductor, you receive a **50% refund**. We arrange return of your parcel.
- **Once your parcel is on the bus:** Cancellation is not available. Your parcel will continue to the destination city. If Patwadi is unable to complete delivery due to an operational failure on our side, we will refund you in full.

To cancel, contact us on WhatsApp with your order ID.

---

## Delays

If the recipient is not available, your delivery partner will make **two attempts at no extra charge**. The second attempt is made within **24 hours** of the first.

If both attempts fail, your parcel is held at **₹100 per day**, starting immediately after the second attempt. There is no grace period. We hold parcels for up to **7 days**. After that, we begin a return to you. **Return freight** is charged at the same rate as your original booking.

While we investigate a serious delay or routing problem, your tracking may show **“Delivery exception — our team is resolving it.”** We will keep you updated by WhatsApp.

---

## Damage

You must report damage within **48 hours** of delivery.

When you claim, send us your order ID and clear photos of the damage. We compare your photos against our custody record — photos taken at every handoff and at delivery — to determine when damage occurred.

Compensation is limited to the **lesser of your declared value or ₹5,000**. This cap applies until cargo insurance is in place. We do not cover indirect loss, business loss, or sentimental value.

Fragile items must be properly packed. If you shipped with packaging we flagged as inadequate, our liability for packaging-related damage is reduced.

---

## Lost Parcels

A parcel is considered **overdue** if it has not been delivered within **48 hours** of the expected arrival time for your corridor.

If your parcel is overdue, contact us immediately. We trace the custody record and work to recover your parcel. If it cannot be recovered, compensation is limited to the **lesser of your declared value or ₹5,000**, plus a **refund of your freight charges**.

Always declare an accurate value at booking. Undervalued parcels may receive less compensation.
