## Full description

Patwadi is intercity parcel delivery built on the corridors that already connect North Indian cities. Instead of treating a parcel like anonymous cargo, Patwadi tracks who has it at every step — from your doorstep, onto the linehaul leg, through the destination city, and to the final recipient. The product is designed for people who need parcels to arrive on a real corridor schedule, with proof at every handoff.

**How Patwadi works**

When you book a parcel, you choose a supported corridor — a defined origin-to-destination route served by scheduled intercity linehaul. Your parcel is collected by a local pickup partner (LMP) in the origin city, handed to the linehaul conductor with a verified code and photo, carried on the corridor trip, received by a local delivery partner in the destination city, and delivered to the recipient. Each transfer is recorded as a custody event: a one-time handoff code plus a mandatory photo. Status in the app is derived from those events, not from guesses or generic tracking pings.

**Custody you can see**

Patwadi sells certainty, not speed. You will not find vague "in transit" labels with no accountability. The customer-facing tracker shows where your parcel is in the corridor journey — picked up, in transit on the corridor, in the destination city, out for delivery, delivered — based on verified handoffs. When something needs your attention (pickup confirmation, delivery scheduling, or an exception our team is resolving), the app tells you in plain language. Internal handoff photos and operator details stay on the operations side; you see the stage and the assurance that each hop was acknowledged.

**Built for North India's corridor reality**

Patwadi is built for the way intercity movement actually works in North India: overnight and scheduled corridor routes between cities, local partners at each end, and corridors that operators already know. We do not pretend to be an instant courier. Parcels move when the corridor moves. That honesty is the point — you book knowing the route, the handoff chain, and what verified means at each step.

**For senders**

- Book intercity parcels on supported corridors from your phone  
- See corridor-based pricing before you pay  
- Confirm pickup in the app when your local partner arrives (code + photo)  
- Track parcel stage from booking through delivery  
- Pay securely via Razorpay; booking is confirmed only after payment  
- Get help through in-app support and WhatsApp when you need a human  

**For operators (LMP and linehaul)**

Patwadi also serves the people who move parcels: local pickup and delivery partners, and linehaul conductors who run corridor trips. Operators see assigned parcels, issue and scan handoff codes, capture proof photos, and manage trips on their approved corridors. Linehaul conductors publish trips with corridor proof, attach parcels, and record custody as parcels move. The app is one operating layer on top of existing corridor relationships — not a replacement for the corridor network.

**Trust infrastructure, not owned fleets**

Patwadi does not own vehicles or employ every driver on the road. The moat is corridor network, trust infrastructure, and an operator network that records custody at every hop. WhatsApp remains a coordination and evidence layer; the app is where handoffs are verified and disputes can be reconstructed. Location is used during active trip and assignment windows as a contingency signal — not as a substitute for custody proof.

**Privacy and account control**

Patwadi collects account information, parcel details, handoff photos, payment metadata, and (for operators) location during active windows — all in service of custody verification and dispute resolution. You can delete your account from Settings; deletion is blocked while you have active in-transit parcels so the evidence chain stays intact. See our privacy policy at https://patwadi.com/docs/privacy-policy

**Who Patwadi is for**

Patwadi is for families sending packages between cities, small businesses moving goods on corridor schedules, and anyone who would rather have verified handoffs than a tracking dot with no accountability. If you need a parcel on the next corridor run with proof at every step, Patwadi is built for that journey.
