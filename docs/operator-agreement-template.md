# Patwadi Operator Agreement (Pilot)

**Between:** Patwadi Logistics LLP ("Patwadi")  
**And:** [Operator legal name] ("Operator")  
**Role:** [ ] Local pickup partner (LMP)  [ ] Linehaul conductor  
**Effective date:** [to be filled]

This agreement governs the Operator's participation in the Patwadi intercity parcel courier network during the closed pilot phase.

---

## 1. Scope of Services

The Operator agrees to handle parcels on the Patwadi network in accordance with this agreement, the Patwadi app workflows, and instructions issued by Patwadi operations.

**LMP operators** may perform customer pickup, transfer to linehaul, receive from linehaul, and final delivery to the customer, as assigned in the app.

**Linehaul operators** may create and operate intercity trips, accept parcel custody at boarding, transport parcels on assigned corridors, and transfer custody at arrival, as recorded in the app.

The Operator must use the Patwadi app for all custody confirmations (handoff code + photo). WhatsApp or verbal confirmations do not replace app-recorded custody.

**Corridor scope.** The Operator is authorised to handle parcels only on corridors explicitly assigned to them by Patwadi. Assigned corridors are recorded in the Patwadi system and may be updated by Patwadi at any time with reasonable notice. Handling parcels on unassigned corridors is outside the scope of this agreement.

---

## 2. Independent Contractor Status and Third-Party Liability

The Operator is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of Patwadi Logistics LLP. Patwadi does not control or direct the route, schedule, speed, vehicle choice, or manner of transport used by the Operator. The Operator uses their own means and equipment to perform services under this agreement.

The Operator is solely responsible for any third-party claims, accidents, personal injuries, property damage, or liabilities arising from their transport activities, including but not limited to incidents involving the bus or vehicle on which parcels are being transported.

The Operator shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Patwadi Logistics LLP, its directors, and employees against any claims, damages, losses, or expenses (including legal fees) arising from the Operator's transport activities or breach of this agreement.

---

## 3. Custody Obligations

The Operator is responsible for parcels while they are in the Operator's custody on the Patwadi network. Custody must be recorded in the Patwadi app at every handoff using the issued verification code and a clear photo of the parcel at the point of transfer.

The Operator must not leave a parcel unattended in an unsecured location. The Operator must not hand a parcel to any person who cannot provide the correct verification code for that handoff step.

The Operator's custody of a parcel begins at the moment they acknowledge a handoff code and complete a custody confirmation (code + photo) in the Patwadi app. Custody ends when the Operator successfully transfers the parcel to the next party in the chain via the same process.

If a custody event in the Patwadi system shows the Operator accepted a parcel, the Operator is accountable for the parcel's condition and location until the next custody event is recorded. The absence of a valid outgoing handoff record (code + photo) will be treated as a custody failure.

The Operator may not complete a handoff without the physical parcel present. Manufacturing or falsifying a handoff record (including using a photo from a previous delivery) is grounds for immediate termination and recovery of losses.

**Fragile items.** The Operator has the right to refuse pickup of any item that appears inadequately packaged, including fragile items without appropriate protective packaging. If the Operator accepts a fragile item with packaging they have noted as inadequate (recorded via the app's packaging acknowledgment), Patwadi's liability for damage from packaging failure is reduced accordingly.

---

## 4. Prohibited Items and Refusal

The Operator must not accept parcels containing prohibited items as defined in Patwadi's Shipping Policy. If the Operator identifies or reasonably suspects a prohibited item, they must refuse custody and notify Patwadi support immediately. Accepting a known prohibited item is a material breach of this agreement.

---

## 5. Payment Terms

Patwadi will settle earnings weekly, every Monday, for parcels with a completed `lmp_to_customer` custody event in the prior 7 days. Settlement is made to the UPI ID or bank account on file in the Operator's KYC record.

Patwadi reserves the right to withhold or deduct from settlement in the following circumstances:

a. A customer dispute or chargeback is under investigation for a delivery the Operator completed.  
b. A parcel was lost or damaged while in the Operator's custody, pending investigation.  
c. A custody failure (missing handoff record, falsified proof) is identified.  
d. The Operator is found to have accepted a prohibited item.

Deductions will not exceed the Operator's earnings for two consecutive settlement periods. Patwadi will notify the Operator of any deduction and its reason within 24 hours of the settlement date.

During the pilot phase: no earnings reserve is held.

Earnings rates and corridor-specific fees are communicated separately by Patwadi operations and may be updated with reasonable notice during the pilot.

---

## 6. Onboarding, KYC, and Account Status

The Operator completes KYC via the Patwadi website before activation. Patwadi admin approves the Operator before operational access is granted. The Operator must keep payment and emergency contact details accurate.

Patwadi may suspend or terminate Operator access if `approval_status` is not approved, if `operator_status` is suspended or inactive, or if the Operator breaches this agreement.

---

## 7. Data and Privacy

The Operator may access customer parcel and address information only as needed to perform assigned services. The Operator must not copy, share, or retain customer data outside the Patwadi app except as required by law.

Location data may be collected during active trips for custody verification and safety. The Operator consents to this collection while performing services on the network.

**Account security.** The Operator's Patwadi account is personal and non-transferable. The Operator may not share their login credentials, handoff codes, or account access with any other person. Custody actions (code issuance, photo submission) performed under the Operator's account are attributed to the Operator regardless of who physically performed them. Account sharing is grounds for immediate termination.

---

## 8. Confidentiality

The Operator must not disclose non-public Patwadi business information, pricing, or operational procedures to third parties without Patwadi's written consent.

---

## 9. Term and Termination

This agreement begins on the effective date and continues until terminated. Either party may terminate with 7 days' written notice during the pilot phase. Patwadi may terminate immediately for material breach, custody failure, falsified records, prohibited item acceptance, or account sharing.

Upon termination, the Operator must complete or safely transfer any parcels still in their custody.

---

## 10. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes will first be addressed through Patwadi operations support. If unresolved, disputes are subject to arbitration under Indian law in [city to be confirmed by lawyer].

## 11. Entire Agreement

This document, together with Patwadi's published Shipping Policy and any written corridor or rate schedules issued by Patwadi, constitutes the agreement between the parties for the pilot phase.
